# Name and terms

## Our name

**Indy Center** is the community: the pilots and controllers on the VATSIM
network who fly and control together.

- Always two words, both capitalized: **Indy Center**.
- There is no long form. Use "Indy Center" everywhere, including formal
  writing.
- The logo shows the name as one word. That join belongs to the logo only;
  in text it is always two words.

| Write | Don't write |
| --- | --- |
| Indy Center | IndyCenter, Indy center, Indy Ctr, vZID |
| Indy Center's events | ZID's events, the ARTCC's events |
| Join Indy Center | Join Indianapolis Center, Join the Indianapolis ARTCC |

## Names that sound alike

Three names come up in Indy Center's world. Each means something different.

| Name | What it is | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Indy Center** | The community | Almost always |
| **Indianapolis ARTCC**, or **ZID** | The VATUSA subdivision Indy Center operates, and the airspace its controllers work and its pilots fly in. ZID is its identifier. | Writing about the airspace or the subdivision: "ZID airspace", "Indianapolis ARTCC's sectors" |
| **Indianapolis Center** | The radio callsign of the enroute positions | Writing what a pilot hears or says on frequency: "contact Indianapolis Center" |

Neither of the last two is a name for the community.

## Brand terms

- **Mark**: the tower on its own, the symbol of Indy Center. White or black.
- **Logo**: the mark with the name beside it. White or black.
- **Special mark**: a version of the mark for an occasion, such as Pride or
  the Kentucky Derby, in its own colors. Used only around its occasion.
- **Partner badge**: the mark or logo with words saying the holder is an
  Indy Center partner, for partners to use on their own channels. It comes
  in two versions: the compact "Indy Center partner" badge and "partnered
  with".
- **Event banner**: the image for one event, posted to VATSIM, VATUSA and
  Discord.

See [Marks](marks.md) for how to use each one.

## Partner terms

These are the words the [partners page](https://flyindycenter.com/partners)
uses.

- **Partner**: a developer, creator or group that Indy Center has a
  partnership with and shows on the partners page. Not "sponsor" or
  "affiliate".
- **Partner category**: the group a partner is listed under, such as
  Developer, Creator or Community.
- **Tagline**: a partner's one-line summary, shown under its name.
- **Call to action**: a labelled button on a partner's page that sends
  visitors somewhere, such as "Visit website" or "Download the scenery".
- **Member perk**: something a partner offers only to Indy Center members,
  such as a discount code. Only signed-in members see it.

See [Partners](partners.md) for how to get a partner page.
