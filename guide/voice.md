# Voice and writing

## How we sound

- **Friendly and welcoming.** Most people reading are new to us, and many are
  new to flight simulation. Write as if you're talking to someone on their first flight.
- **Accurate about ATC, without walls of jargon.** Use the right terms, and
  explain the ones a newcomer won't know the first time they appear.
- **Clear that it's a simulation.** Indy Center is a community on the VATSIM
  network, not a real air traffic control facility. Don't write anything
  that could be seen as real-world advice or guidance.

## Names

- Write **Indy Center** for the community, every time. See
  [Name and terms](name-and-terms.md) for the names that sound alike.
- Write **positions for pilots as they appear on the network**: IND_CTR, IND_TWR,
  CVG_APP.
- Write **positions for controllers as they appear in the SOP**: FLM 83, CVG LCW, LEX RE
- Write **staff titles** exactly as the [staff page](https://flyindycenter.com/staff)
  shows them.\*\* Titles are the most common mistake, so check before you post.

## Times

- **On the website and in Discord,** times show in each reader's own time
  zone. In Discord, use its timestamp tags (`<t:1769644740:F>`) so this
  happens for you. [Hammertime](https://hammertime.cyou/en) can generate them for you.
- **Anywhere that can't convert times** (images, banners, stream overlays,
  documents), write them in **Zulu with a "Z"**: 2300Z. Add the local time
  after it if it helps, as event banners do: "2300–0300Z | 7:00–11:00 PM ET".
- An event that starts at **midnight Zulu** may be written as **2359Z the day
  before**, so the Zulu date matches the date most of our members see that
  evening.

## The disclaimer

Wherever something could be taken for real-world guidance (material for
pilots to use, or tips on how to control), include this disclaimer:

> Indy Center is not affiliated with the Federal Aviation Administration or
> any governing aviation body. This material is for use on the VATSIM network
> only.
