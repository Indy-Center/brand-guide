# Indy Center brand guide

How to show and write about Indy Center, the pilot and controller community
on the VATSIM network, and the files to do it with.

It's for anyone who talks about the community: Indy Center staff and
members making graphics and posts, the events team, and our partners, both
on their page at [flyindycenter.com/partners](https://flyindycenter.com/partners)
and on their own channels.

The guide is short on purpose. Where it doesn't answer a question, ask the
brand owner: the ATM, at [atm@flyindycenter.com](mailto:atm@flyindycenter.com).
