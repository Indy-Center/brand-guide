# Partner badge

For partners to show they are an Indy Center partner, in two versions, each
in white and black. Use white on dark backgrounds and black on light ones.

- **Partner badge:** the mark and "INDY CENTER PARTNER" in a rounded outline.
  Compact, for overlays, panels and store pages.
- **Partnered with:** "PARTNERED WITH" above the logo. For larger spaces,
  such as a credits screen or a website footer.
